# Chain Rule vs Product Rule Confusion

Students sometimes apply the product rule to a nested function.

## Correction

Use the chain rule for compositions such as sin(x^2).
Use the product rule for products such as x^2 * sin(x).

## Instructional note

Compare one nested example and one product example side by side.
